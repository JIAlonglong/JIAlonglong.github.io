PAL (Predictive Action-conditioned Locomotion) uses a compact world model to anticipate how motor actions affect a quadruped's near-future motion and terrain interaction. These predictions provide context for a reinforcement-learning policy, while teacher–student learning transfers the predictive representation to onboard depth and proprioceptive sensing. The video demonstrates simulated parkour, baseline comparisons, and real-world multi-terrain locomotion on the Unitree Go2.
